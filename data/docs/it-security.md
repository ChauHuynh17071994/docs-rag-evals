# IT Security Rules

## Passwords and MFA

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the ERP system, and the VPN.

## Lost or stolen devices

A lost or stolen laptop or phone must be reported to the IT service desk within 2 hours. The service desk can be reached at extension 4000 or through the self-service portal.

## Software installation

Employees may only install software from the company software catalogue. Requests for other software are reviewed by IT security within 5 working days.

## Phishing

Suspicious emails should be reported with the "Report phishing" button in the mail client. Do not forward suspicious emails to colleagues.
