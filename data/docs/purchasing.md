# Purchasing Process

## Purchase requisitions

Every purchase above 500 euros needs a purchase requisition in the ERP system. Purchases below 500 euros can be paid with a company card.

## Approval limits

Team leads can approve requisitions up to 5,000 euros. Department heads can approve up to 50,000 euros. Anything above 50,000 euros requires approval from the CFO.

## Suppliers

New suppliers must be registered by the procurement team before the first order. Registration takes about 3 working days and requires the supplier's VAT number and bank details.

## Invoices

Supplier invoices are paid 30 days net. Invoices must reference a valid purchase order number, otherwise they are returned to the supplier.
