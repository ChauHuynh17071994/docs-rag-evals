# Leave Policy

## Annual leave

Full-time employees earn 2.5 days of annual leave per full month of employment, which is 30 days per year. Summer leave of at least 12 consecutive days should be taken between June and August.

## Sick leave

Employees must inform their manager of sick leave on the first morning of absence. A doctor's or nurse's certificate is required for absences longer than 3 days.

## Parental leave

Parental leave follows Finnish law. Employees should notify HR at least 2 months before parental leave begins.
