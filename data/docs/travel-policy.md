# Travel and Expense Policy

Northwind Logistics is a fictional company used for this demo corpus.

## Booking travel

All business trips must be booked through the internal travel portal at least 14 days before departure. Trips booked later than that require written approval from the employee's line manager.

Economy class is the default for flights under 6 hours. Business class may be used for flights longer than 6 hours if approved by a department head.

## Daily allowance

Employees travelling abroad receive a daily allowance of 52 euros for meals. Domestic trips longer than 10 hours receive a daily allowance of 26 euros.

## Submitting expenses

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip. Every claim must include a photo or PDF of the receipt. Claims without receipts are only reimbursed up to 20 euros.

## Hotels

The maximum hotel rate is 160 euros per night in capital cities and 120 euros per night elsewhere.
