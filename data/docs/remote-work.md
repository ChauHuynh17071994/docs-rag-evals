# Remote Work Guidelines

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probation period may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Warehouse and driving roles are not eligible for remote work.

## Core hours

Remote employees must be reachable on chat between 9:00 and 15:00 Helsinki time on working days.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop, a monitor, and a headset. Employees can claim up to 300 euros once every 3 years for a desk chair or other home office furniture.

## Working abroad

Working remotely from outside Finland is allowed for at most 20 working days per calendar year and must be approved by HR in advance because of tax and insurance rules.
